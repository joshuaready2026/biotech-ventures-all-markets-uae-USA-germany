Compare and contrast

Traditional psychedelics vs. the NeuroForge™ approach

People, including many veterans, already go to great lengths for these compounds. The traditional route is hard. Ours is designed to be different.

Traditional psychedelics

Ibogaine · Psilocybin · LSD · MDMA · Ketamine

  • Most are controlled substances in the U.S.
  • People often have to leave the country, commonly for Mexico, at high cost
  • Intense, long, and sometimes dark or frightening experiences
  • Hours of doctor supervision and extra recovery time
  • Missed work and disruption to family life

The NeuroForge™ approach

Research-tool platform

  • Designed not to be a controlled substance
  • Built for laboratory research, including in the United States and the globe
  • Designed to study the same neuroplasticity biology
  • Designed with no trip: non-hallucinogenic and non-intoxicating
  • Practical for research today, and for partners who develop it further

Research-use-only laboratory tools, not a medicine. No treatment or efficacy claims. Non-hallucinogenic is a design aim, not a result shown in people.
